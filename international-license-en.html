HaleLea Car Rental accepts International Driving Permits (IDPs). Please check the requirements and necessary documents for driving in Japan before visiting us.

Required Documents

Required Documents for Rental

Please bring original documents when visiting us. Copies or photos are not accepted.

  • Original International Driving Permit (IDP) under the 1949 Geneva Convention
  • Original Passport of the Driver
  • Original Driver’s License from Your Home Country
  • Customers with a driver’s license from Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Monaco, or Taiwan must bring an official Japanese translation issued by an authorized organization.
  • Your passport must have an entry stamp or official proof confirming your date of entry into Japan.

Eligibility

Driving Eligibility in Japan

Driving Requirements in Japan (Must meet one of the following):

01

IDP

Holders of an International Driving Permit (IDP) under the 1949 Geneva Convention

02

Your Passport

Passport required with Japan entry stamp.

03

Home Country Driver’s License

Valid original home country license required alongside IDP.

04

Document Verification / Document Check

After your documents are verified, you may use the rental car.

Translation

Countries Eligible via Japanese Translation

Holders of driver’s licenses from the following countries and regions may drive in Japan by presenting an official Japanese translation issued by a designated organization alongside their original license.

  • Switzerland
  • Germany
  • France
  • Belgium
  • Monaco
  • Taiwan
Important

Invalid International Driving Permits (IDPs) / IDPs Not Accepted

Only International Driving Permits (IDPs) issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention are valid in Japan.

  • 1926 Paris Convention IDP
  • 1943 Washington Convention IDP
  • 1968 Vienna Convention IDP
  • Expired International Driving Permits (IDPs)
  • Copies, Photos, or Electronic Data Only
Validity

IDP Expiration Date

Please check the expiration date before use.

  • IDP validity: 1 year from date of issue.
  • In Japan, you may drive for up to one year from your date of entry, or until the expiration date of your International Driving Permit (IDP), whichever comes first.
  • We cannot rent vehicles to customers with an expired IDP.
  • Our staff will verify your documents upon your arrival.
Passport

Passport Requirements

Please be sure to bring your original passport.

  • Passport showing Japan entry date required.
  • If you use automated gates, we may need to verify your entry stamp or other documentation.
  • Passport copies/photos not accepted for rental.
  • Required: IDP, home country license, and passport (all 3 required).
Rental Conditions

Halelea Car Rental Terms and Conditions

Please review the following before your rental for a safe experience.

  • Please present the required documents for all drivers.
  • Please bring the originals of all three documents: your IDP, home country driver's license, and passport.
  • Document verification may take some time.
  • We will refuse vehicle rentals in the event of drunk driving, dangerous driving, or any traffic violations.
  • Please comply with our rental terms and conditions as well as all traffic regulations.
  • Please feel free to ask our staff if you have any questions.
FAQ

FAQ

Can I rent a car with only an International Driving Permit?

No, you cannot. In addition to your International Driving Permit (IDP), you must present your passport and your home country's driver's license (originals only).

Can I use a copy or a photo on my smartphone?

They cannot be used. Please present original documents for all required items.

Can I use a Taiwanese driver's license?

Yes, you can use it by providing an official Japanese translation.

Can I use an International Driving Permit issued under the Vienna Convention?

They cannot be used. In Japan, only International Driving Permits issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention are valid.

How long is an International Driving Permit valid for?

It is valid for one year from the date of issue. Within Japan, the period you can legally drive may vary depending on your date of entry and other factors.
