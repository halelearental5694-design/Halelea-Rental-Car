Privacy Policy

At Halelea Car Rental, we are committed to properly protecting and managing your personal information so that you can use our services with complete peace of mind.

Personal Information Protection Policy

Privacy Policy

Incredible Japan Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") recognizes the importance of personal information of customers using Halelea Car Rental (hereinafter referred to as "the Service"). The Company complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other relevant laws and regulations, and manages and operates such information appropriately in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Collection of Personal Information

We collect personal information, including your name, address, telephone number, email address, driver's license information, and other necessary details, strictly within the scope required to process car rental reservations, respond to inquiries, and provide various services.

2. Purpose of Use

The personal information we collect will be used for the following purposes:

  • Car rental reservations and vehicle rental operations
  • Identity verification and verification of driving eligibility
  • Handling and addressing customer inquiries, requests, and feedback
  • Contacting customers regarding lost and found items
  • Handling accidents and insurance claims
  • Compliance with laws and regulations
  • Analyzing and improving service quality

3. Management of Personal Information

We implement appropriate security measures to manage your personal information in order to prevent unauthorized access, leakage, alteration, or loss.

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

We will not provide your personal information to any third party without your consent, except as required by laws and regulations. However, this does not apply when disclosure is necessary for accident response or to insurance companies, police, public offices, or other administrative bodies.

5. Use of Cookies and Other Technologies

This website may use cookies and other similar technologies for the purpose of improving our services and analyzing traffic. Information collected through cookies does not include any personally identifiable information.

6. Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion of Personal Information

When a customer requests the disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, or suspension of use of their personal information, we will verify the identity of the individual and respond appropriately in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is subject to change without prior notice in accordance with amendments to laws and regulations or changes in service content. The latest version will always be published on this page.
