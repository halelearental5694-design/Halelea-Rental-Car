Chapter 1 General Provisions

Article 1: The Company shall lease the rental vehicle (hereinafter referred to as the "Rental Car") to the renter (including drivers; the same shall apply hereinafter) in accordance with the provisions of these Terms and Conditions, and the renter shall lease the same.

Any matters not provided for in these Terms and Conditions shall be governed by laws, regulations, or general custom.

2. The Company may enter into special agreements to the extent that they do not violate the intent of these Terms and Conditions, laws and regulations, or general custom. In the event a special agreement is executed, such special agreement shall take precedence.

Chapter 2 Rental Agreement

Article 2 (Reservations)

When making a reservation, the renter may specify in advance the vehicle model, rental start date and time, pick-up location, rental period, return location, designated drivers, and any other rental conditions. The Company shall accept such reservations within the scope of its available rental fleet.

2. Reservations specified in the preceding paragraph shall be made upon payment of the reservation deposit stipulated separately by the Company.

3. If the procedure to finalize the rental contract is not started within one hour after the reserved pickup time, the reservation will be considered canceled.

4. If you wish to cancel or modify your reservation mentioned in Paragraph 1, you must obtain prior approval from the Company.

Article 3 (Execution of Rental Agreement)

The Company will enter into a Rental Agreement upon the renter's request, unless no vehicles are available or the renter meets any of the conditions specified in Article 9.

When finalizing the agreement, the Company may request to see your driver's license, an additional ID, and a mobile phone number to contact you during your rental. We may also make copies of your license and submitted documents.

2. Applications for the Rental Agreement shall be made by specifying the rental conditions stipulated in Paragraph 1 of the preceding Article.

3. Upon execution of the Rental Agreement, the Company shall charge the rental fee specified separately.

Article 4 (Formation of Rental Agreement, etc.)

The Rental Agreement shall take effect when the Company receives the rental fee and delivers the rental car to the renter. In this case, the reservation deposit shall be applied toward part of the rental fee.

Article 5 (Cancellation of Rental Agreement)

If the renter falls under any of the following items during the rental period, the Company may terminate the Rental Agreement without any notice or demand, and immediately demand the return of the rental car. In such case, the Company shall not refund the rental fee received pursuant to Article 4. However, if a special agreement provides for deferred payment, or if there are unpaid balances due to an extension of the rental period or other reasons, the renter shall pay such fees.

  • (1) In the event of a breach of these Terms and Conditions.
  • (2) If a traffic accident occurs due to the renter's fault.
  • (3) In the event that the renter comes to fall under any of the items in Article 9.

2．If the rental car becomes unusable due to a defect that existed prior to its delivery to the renter, the renter may terminate the Rental Agreement, except where measures pursuant to Article 22, Paragraph 3 have been taken.

Article 6 (Early Termination of Rental Agreement Due to Force Majeure)

If the rental car becomes unusable during the rental period due to natural disasters or other events of force majeure, the Rental Agreement shall terminate.

2. In the event that the provisions of the preceding paragraph apply, the renter shall notify the Company to that effect.

Article 7 (Mid-Term Cancellation)）

The renter may terminate the Rental Agreement even during the rental period, subject to obtaining the consent of the Company. In such case, the renter shall pay the mid-term cancellation fee specified in Article 25.

2. If the rental car is returned during the rental period due to a rental car accident or breakdown attributable to the renter, the Rental Agreement shall be deemed terminated.

3. When the renter returns the rental car pursuant to the preceding paragraph, the Company shall not refund the rental fee received pursuant to Article 4.

Article 8 (Changes to Rental Terms and Conditions)

If the renter wishes to change the rental conditions specified in Article 3, Paragraph 2 after the Rental Agreement has been formed, the renter shall obtain the prior consent of the Company.

2. The Company may refuse to consent to the change in rental conditions pursuant to the preceding paragraph if such change interferes with its rental operations.

Article 9 (Refusal to Execute Rental Agreement)

The Company may refuse to execute the Rental Agreement if the renter falls under any of the following items.

  • (1) When the renter does not hold a driver's license necessary to operate the rented car.
  • (2) When the renter is under the influence of alcohol.
  • (3) When the renter exhibits symptoms of intoxication from narcotics, stimulants, thinners, or other substances.
  • (4) When the driver at the time of vehicle delivery differs from the driver specified at the time of reservation.
  • (5) When the renter has defaulted on the payment of rental fees for past rentals.
  • (6) When the renter engaged in any act falling under any of the items in Article 17 during past rentals.
  • (7) When the renter accommodates an infant under 6 years of age as a passenger without using a child safety seat.
  • (8) In addition to the preceding items, when the Company deems the rental of the car to be inappropriate.

Chapter 3 Rental Vehicles

Article 10 (Start Date and Time, etc.)

The Company shall rent the rental car specified in Article 1 at the start date and time and the pickup location specified in Article 3, Paragraph 2.

Article 11 (Rental Method, etc.)

The Company shall rent the rental car after confirming, jointly with the renter, that there are no defects in maintenance or other issues, through the routine inspection and maintenance specified in Article 47-2 of the Road Transport Vehicle Act, as well as the inspection of the exterior body and accessories based on a separately established inspection checklist.

2. If any defects in maintenance or other issues are discovered during the confirmation set forth in the preceding paragraph, the Company shall take necessary measures, such as vehicle replacement.

3. Upon delivery of the rental car, the Company shall issue to the renter a prescribed Rental Certificate containing the matters specified by the Director of the District Transport Bureau / Transport Branch Office.

4. The renter shall properly install the child safety seat at their own responsibility, and the renter shall assume all responsibilities and liabilities associated therewith.

Article 12 (Rental Fees)

The rental fee set forth in Article 4 to be received by the Company shall be based on the fee schedule filed with and implemented by the District Transport Bureau at the time of rental.

2. The amount of the rental fees received by the Company shall be the total of the basic rental fee and any optional fees associated with the rental. If additional charges—such as extension fees, deductible amounts for accidents, or non-operation charges (NOC)—arise beyond the fees already received upon return of the rental car, such charges shall be settled at the time of return.

Chapter 4 Rental Charges

Article 13 (Measures Accompanying Revision of Rental Fees)

If the rental fees set forth in the preceding Article are revised after a reservation has been made pursuant to Article 2, the fee schedule applied at the time of the reservation shall apply, notwithstanding Paragraph 1 of the preceding Article.

Chapter 5 Responsibility

Article 14 (Periodic Inspection and Maintenance)

The Company shall rent rental cars that have undergone periodic inspection and maintenance under Article 48 of the Road Transport Vehicle Act.

Article 15 (Routine Inspection and Maintenance)

The renter shall perform routine inspection and maintenance specified in Article 47-2 of the Road Transport Vehicle Act for the rented car prior to daily operation during the rental period.

Article 16 (Management Responsibility of the Renter)

The renter shall use and manage the rental car with the care of a good manager.

2. The management responsibility set forth in the preceding paragraph shall commence upon delivery of the rental car and shall terminate upon return thereof to the Company.

Article 17 (Prohibited Acts)

The renter shall not engage in any of the following acts during the rental period.

  • (1) Using the rental car for motor transportation business or similar purposes without obtaining the consent of the Company and the permits, approvals, or other authorizations required under the Road Transportation Act.
  • (2) Subleasing the rental car, providing it as collateral, or engaging in any other acts that infringe upon the Company's ownership rights.
  • (3) Forging or altering the license plate of the rental car, or altering its original state by modifying, customizing, or making any other alterations to the rental car.
  • (4) Using the rental car for various tests or competitions, or using it to tow or push other vehicles, without obtaining the consent of the Company.
  • (5) Using the rental car in violation of laws and regulations or public order and morals.
  • (6) Engaging in any of the following acts without obtaining the consent of the Company:
    • (i) Allowing any person other than the renter and the driver designated at the time of concluding the rental agreement to drive the rental car.
    • (ii) Purchasing non-life insurance for the rental car.
    • (iii) Allowing pets in the rental car without obtaining the consent of the Company, or releasing pets from their cages inside the vehicle even if such consent has been obtained.

Article 18 (Duty to Carry the Rental Certificate, etc.)

The renter shall carry the rental certificate issued pursuant to Article 11, Paragraph 3 at all times during the rental period of the rental car.

2. If the renter loses the rental certificate, the renter shall immediately notify the Company to that effect.

Article 19 (Liability for Damages)

If the renter causes damage to a third party or to the Company through the use of the rental car, the renter shall be liable for compensating such damage; provided, however, that this shall not apply if the damage is due to reasons not attributable to the renter.

2. If the renter damages the rental car or its accessories due to an accident attributable to the renter, the renter shall pay to the Company the damages (Non-Operation Charge) separately prescribed by the Company as compensation for business loss during the repair period of the rental car or its accessories.

Chapter 6: Procedures Following a Motor Vehicle Accident, etc.

Article 20 (Handling of Accidents)

If an accident involving the rental car occurs during the rental period, the renter shall take all measures required by laws and regulations regardless of the scale of the accident, and shall handle the matter in accordance with the following provisions.

  • (1) Immediately report the details and circumstances of the accident to the Company.
  • (2) Submit documents or evidence required by the Company and the insurance company regarding the accident.
  • (3) Obtain the prior consent of the Company when entering into a settlement or agreement with a third party.
  • (4) Have repairs to the rental car performed by the Company or at a repair facility designated by the Company.

2. The renter shall endeavor to resolve the accident on their own responsibility.

3. The Company shall provide advice regarding the handling of the accident and cooperate in its resolution.

Article 21 (Compensation)

The Company shall cover the liability for damages incurred by the renter under Article 19, Paragraph 2 within the following limits, under the non-life insurance contract concluded for the rental car and the compensation system established by the Company:

  • (1) Bodily Injury Liability: Unlimited per person (including Compulsory Automobile Liability Insurance)
  • (2) Property Damage Liability: Unlimited per accident (no deductible)
  • (3) Personal Injury Protection: Up to 30 million yen per person

2. Any damages exceeding the compensation limits set forth in the preceding paragraph shall be borne by the renter.

3. If the Company pays damages to be borne by the renter in excess of the compensation limits set forth in Paragraph 1, the renter shall immediately reimburse the Company for such excess amount.

4. Non-life insurance and this compensation system shall not apply to damages caused by any of the following: accidents not reported to the police and the Company; accidents falling under the disclaimer clauses of the non-life insurance policy; accidents occurring after the rental that fall under any of the items of Article 9 or Article 17; or accidents occurring after the rental period has been extended without the consent of the Company.

Article 22 (Measures for Breakdown, etc.)

If the renter discovers any abnormality or breakdown of the rental car during the rental period, the renter shall immediately stop driving, contact the Company, and follow the instructions of the Company.

2. If any abnormality or breakdown of the rental car is due to the intentional act or negligence of the renter, the renter shall bear the costs required for the retrieval and repair of the rental car.

3. If the rental car becomes unusable due to a defect that existed prior to the rental, the renter may receive the provision of a substitute rental car or other equivalent measures from the Company.

4. Except for the measures specified in the preceding paragraph, the renter shall not make any claim against the Company for damages arising from the inability to use the rental car.

Article 23 (Exemption from Liability Due to Force Majeure)

If the renter becomes unable to return the rental car within the rental period due to a natural disaster or other force majeure event, the Company shall not hold the renter liable for any damages arising therefrom.

In such case, the renter shall immediately contact the Company and follow its instructions.

2. If the Company becomes unable to provide the rental car or a substitute rental car due to a natural disaster or other force majeure event, the renter shall not hold the Company liable for any damages arising therefrom.

In such case, the Company shall immediately notify the renter.

Chapter 7 Cancellation, Refunds, etc.

Article 24 (Cancellation of Reservation, etc.)

If the renter, after making a reservation pursuant to Article 2, cancels the reservation for the renter's own reasons, or fails to execute a Rental Contract even after one hour or more has elapsed past the scheduled rental time, the renter shall pay a reservation cancellation fee as separately stipulated.

If the Company has received a reservation deposit, such reservation deposit shall be offset against the reservation cancellation fee.

2. If the Company, despite accepting a reservation pursuant to Article 2, cancels the reservation or fails to execute a Rental Contract for its own reasons, the Company shall refund the reservation deposit.

3. If a Rental Contract is not executed for reasons other than those provided in the preceding two paragraphs despite a reservation having been made pursuant to Article 2, the reservation shall be deemed to have been canceled. In such case, the Company shall refund the amount of the reservation deposit after deducting the reservation cancellation fee.

4. Except for the cases provided in the preceding three paragraphs, neither the Company nor the renter shall make any claim against the other regarding the failure to execute a Rental Contract.

Article 25 (Mid-Term Cancellation Fee)

When the renter effects a mid-term cancellation pursuant to Article 7, Paragraph 1, the renter shall pay the rental charges corresponding to the period up to the cancellation, as well as the following mid-term cancellation fee.

Mid-Term Cancellation Fee = {(Rental Charges corresponding to the total Rental Contract period) - (Rental Charges corresponding to the period from the start of the rental to the cancellation)} × 50%

Article 26 (Refund of Rental Charges)

When any of the following items applies, the Company shall refund all or part of the rental charges received from the renter in accordance with the provisions set forth in each respective item.

  • (1) If the Renter terminates the Rental Contract pursuant to Article 5, Paragraph 2, the full amount of the rental fees received.
  • (2) If the Rental Contract is terminated pursuant to Article 6, Paragraph 1, the balance remaining after deducting the rental fees corresponding to the period from the start of the rental to the termination of the Rental Contract from the rental fees received.
  • (3) If the Renter cancels the contract mid-term pursuant to Article 7, Paragraph 1, the balance remaining after deducting the rental fees corresponding to the period from the start of the rental to the return of the vehicle upon mid-term cancellation from the rental fees received.

2. Upon making the refund referred to in the preceding paragraph, if there are mid-term cancellation fees or any other amounts to be received by the Company, such amounts may be set off against the refund.

Chapter 8 Return

Article 27 Inspection of the Rental Vehicle, etc.

When returning the Rental Vehicle to the Company, the Renter shall return it in the condition confirmed at the time of delivery, fair wear and tear excepted.

Upon return of the Rental Vehicle, the Company shall check the condition of the Rental Vehicle in the presence of the Renter.

3．Upon return of the Rental Vehicle, the Renter shall, in the presence of the Company, return the vehicle after ensuring that no personal belongings of the Renter or any passengers are left inside the Rental Vehicle. The Company shall bear no responsibility for any personal belongings left behind after the return of the vehicle.

Article 28 (Return Period of the Rental Vehicle, etc.)

The Renter shall return the Rental Vehicle within the rental period.

2．If the Renter extends the rental period pursuant to Article 8, Paragraph 1, the Renter shall pay either the rental fee corresponding to the modified rental period, or the total of the original rental fee plus the excess charge, whichever is lower.

3．Notwithstanding Article 8, Paragraph 1, if the Renter returns the Rental Vehicle after exceeding the rental period without obtaining the approval of the Company, the Renter shall pay a special extension fee calculated in accordance with the provisions set forth below.

Special Extension Fee = Number of Overdue Hours × Overdue Fee Unit Rate × 300%

Article 29 (Return Location, etc.)

If the Renter or the Driver changes the designated return location pursuant to Article 3, Paragraph 2, the Renter or the Driver shall bear the expenses for forwarding required as a result of such change in return location.

2．If the Renter or the Driver returns the Rental Vehicle to a location other than the designated return location without obtaining the approval of the Company pursuant to Article 8, Paragraph 1, the Renter or the Driver shall pay a Penalty for Change of Return Location as set forth below.

Penalty for Change of Return Location = Expenses for Forwarding Required Due to the Change of Return Location × 300%

Article 30 (Payment When the Fuel Tank Is Not Full)

If the fuel tank is not full upon return of the Rental Vehicle, the Renter shall pay for the fuel in accordance with the rates separately established by the Company.

Article 31 (Measures in the Event of Non-Return of the Rental Vehicle）

If the Renter fails to return the Rental Vehicle to the return location specified in Article 29, Paragraph 1 even after 72 hours have elapsed from the time of expiration of the rental period, and fails to respond to the Company's demand for return, or if the whereabouts of the Renter are unknown, the Company shall take necessary legal procedures.

The Renter shall separately pay to the Company any consumption tax levied on monetary obligations under these Terms and Conditions.

Article 33 (Late Payment Interest)

If the Renter fails to perform a monetary obligation under these Terms and Conditions, the Renter shall pay to the Company late payment interest at a rate of 14.6% per annum.

Article 34 (Use of Personal Information)

The Renter or the Driver shall agree that the Company uses personal information for the purpose of verifying the customer's identity and conducting screening.

Article 35 (Detailed Rules of Contract)

The Company may separately establish detailed regulations for the implementation of these Terms and Conditions.

2．When the Company has established detailed regulations separately, the Company shall post them at each of its branches, as well as state them in the brochures and price lists issued by the Company. The same shall apply when these regulations are modified.

Article 36 (Jurisdiction)

In the event of a dispute arising regarding rights and obligations under these Terms and Conditions, the district court or summary court having jurisdiction over the location of the Company's head office shall be the court of exclusive jurisdiction.
