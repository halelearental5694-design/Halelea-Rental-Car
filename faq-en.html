FAQ

Here are answers to frequently asked questions prior to making a reservation or picking up your vehicle. If you have any other questions not covered here, please feel free to contact us via LINE or phone.

RESERVATION

About Reservations

Frequently Asked Questions About Reservations

Can I make a same-day reservation?

Yes, same-day reservations are available if vehicles are in stock. Please contact us first via LINE or phone to check availability.

How far in advance can I make a reservation?

Reservations can be made several months in advance. We highly recommend booking early for peak seasons.

Can I change my reservation?

Yes, you can. However, changes may not be possible depending on vehicle availability, so please contact us as early as possible.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes. If you need to cancel, please inform us as early as possible.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

If you know your flight will be delayed, please contact us. We will do our best to accommodate you.

Can I make a reservation outside of business hours?

You can send reservations and inquiries via LINE 24/7. We will respond sequentially during our business hours.

RENTAL

How to Use Our Service

Do I need anything other than my driver's license?

Customers with a Japanese driver's license must bring a valid license. If you are using an International Driving Permit (IDP), you will also need your passport and your home country's driver's license.

Is airport transfer available?

We offer paid shuttle service to and from Ishigaki Airport. Reservations are required in advance, so please let us know when making your booking.

Do you provide shuttle service to the Ferry Terminal?

Yes, this service is available for an additional fee. If you would like to request it, please contact us in advance.

What are your business hours?

Business hours are from 8:00 to 17:00.

Can I pick up or return a car outside of business hours?

Subject to availability and conditions. Please inquire in advance.

Is it safe to drive in Ishigaki for the first time?

Ishigaki Island has relatively easy-to-drive roads, so even first-time visitors can enjoy driving with peace of mind. Our staff will explain the vehicle controls and key precautions before you depart.

VEHICLE

Vehicles & Equipment

Questions About Vehicles & Equipment

What types of vehicles do you have?

We offer compact cars (kei cars), vans, and three-wheeled trikes. Please see our Vehicles page for details.

Are the vehicles non-smoking?

Yes, all of our vehicles are non-smoking. Please refrain from smoking inside the vehicles, including electronic cigarettes.

Do the cars come with a GPS navigation system?

GPS navigation is available in select vehicles. Please inquire when making your reservation.

Is Bluetooth available in the cars?

In Bluetooth-equipped vehicles, you can connect your smartphone to play music and make hands-free calls.

Can I charge my smartphone in the car?

Some of our vehicles are equipped with USB ports. Please bring your own charging cable if needed.

Are child safety seats available for rent?

They are available for rent at 1,000 yen per seat. As quantities are limited, please make a reservation in advance.

Do you also have booster seats available?

Yes, booster seats are available. Please let us know the number you need when making your reservation.

Are pets allowed in the vehicles?

Please consult with us in advance. We may be able to accommodate your request depending on vehicle availability.

Can I drive a three-wheeled trike with a standard driver's license?

You can drive with a standard driver's license (AT/automatic-only license allowed). While helmets are not legally required, we ask that you drive safely.

INSURANCE

Insurance & Coverage

Questions regarding insurance coverage and accident procedures

Should I sign up for an optional coverage plan?

To protect against unexpected accidents or trouble, we recommend enrolling in either our Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) or Premium Coverage plan.

What is Premium Coverage?

This plan provides comprehensive coverage that builds on the standard Collision Damage Waiver (CDW), offering you even greater peace of mind. For more details, please visit our Coverage Plans page.

What should I do if I get into an accident?

1. Administer first aid to any injured persons. 2. Call 119 (Ambulance/Fire) and 110 (Police). 3. Contact HaleLea Rent-a-Car.

What should I do if the vehicle breaks down?

Please park the vehicle in a safe location and stop driving immediately. After doing so, please contact us.

Can I settle the matter directly with the other party?

No. Making a private settlement may affect your insurance coverage. In the event of an accident, you must report it to the police and contact us immediately.

INTERNATIONAL

About International Driving Permits

This information is for guests visiting from overseas.

Can I rent a vehicle with only an International Driving Permit (IDP)?

No. In addition to your International Driving Permit (IDP), you will need your passport and original driver's license from your home country.

Can I use a Taiwanese driver's license?

Yes, you can use your license as long as you bring an official Japanese translation issued by an authorized organization.

Can I use a photocopy or a photo on my smartphone?

No. Copies and photos are not accepted. Please present the original documents for all required items.

Are there any specific requirements for the International Driving Permit (IDP)?

In Japan, only International Driving Permits (IDPs) issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention are valid.

Where can I check the detailed requirements?

For details regarding required documents, eligible countries, and expiration dates, please check our International Driving Permit page.

RETURN

Returns and Payment

Questions Regarding Return Procedures and Payment

Do I need to refill the gas tank before returning the vehicle?

Yes. For gasoline-powered vehicles, please fill up the tank before returning, and present your gas receipt at return. Note that three-wheeled trikes include fuel costs, so a full tank return is not required; however, please add at least 1 liter of fuel if the low fuel light comes on.

What should I do if I am going to be late returning the vehicle?

Please call us as soon as you know you will be late. Depending on subsequent reservations, we may not be able to extend your rental period.。

Can I extend my rental period?

Extensions are possible as long as the vehicle is available. Please be sure to contact us before your scheduled return time.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit cards, PayPay, Rakuten Pay, au PAY, QUICPay, and Japanese transit IC cards.

Can I get a receipt?

Yes, we issue tax-qualified receipts compliant with the Japanese invoice system. If you need one, please let our staff know during check-in or when returning the vehicle.
