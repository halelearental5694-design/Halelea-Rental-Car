Other Insurance

About the Rider for Driving Other Vehicles

If you have an auto insurance policy that includes coverage for driving other vehicles, your own insurance policy may apply.

Coverage details vary by insurance company, so please check with your insurance provider before use.

Please feel free to consult our staff if you have any questions.

Not Covered

Main cases not covered by compensation

  • Drunk driving / Driving under the influence of alcohol
  • Driving without a license
  • Accidents or damage caused intentionally
  • Unauthorized driving by someone other than the contract holder
  • Puncture / Tire Damage
  • Damage to the wheel only
  • Lost or Stolen Key
  • Significant soiling or unpleasant odors inside the vehicle
  • Putting the wrong type of fuel in the tank
  • Accidents in areas where driving is prohibited, such as coastlines, sandy beaches, and riverbeds.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Compensation

Should I get the coverage?

To ensure you can enjoy your trip with peace of mind, we recommend enrolling in the deductible waiver or premium coverage.

What is an NOC?

This is compensation for business interruption during the period when operations could not be conducted due to an accident or breakdown.

Can I use the coverage for driving other vehicles?

This may be applicable depending on the details of your policy. Please check with your insurance company in advance.

Are flat tires covered?

Damage to tires and wheels, as well as punctures, are not covered.

Can I sign up for coverage on the day itself?

You can enroll in the coverage plan before departure.
