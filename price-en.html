FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Half Day Plan?

The Half Day Plan is available from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM or from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

Can I make a same-day reservation?

Yes. Same-day reservations are available depending on vehicle availability. Please contact us via LINE or phone.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit cards, electronic money, and QR code payments.

Do you recommend adding CDW?

Yes. We recommend adding optional coverage so you can enjoy your trip around Ishigaki Island with greater peace of mind.

Can I return the vehicle after business hours?

Vehicles must generally be returned during our business hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
