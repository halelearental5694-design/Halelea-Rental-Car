FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make a same-day reservation?

Yes. Same-day reservations are available depending on vehicle availability.

Do you provide airport or ferry terminal pickup?

Paid pickup service is available from New Ishigaki Airport and the Remote Island Ferry Terminal. Please contact us in advance.

Do you offer child seats?

Yes. Child seats are available for ¥1,000 each. Advance reservation is required.

Can I rent with an IDP?

Yes. Customers with a valid IDP are welcome.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit cards, transportation IC cards, PayPay, Rakuten Pay, au PAY and QUICPay.

Can I use my own automobile insurance?

If your automobile insurance includes coverage for driving other vehicles, it may also apply to your rental vehicle. Please contact us for details.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation is free until 6:00 PM two days before your reservation. A 50% cancellation fee applies the day before, and a 100% fee applies on the day of rental. No cancellation fee will be charged for flight cancellations or severe weather such as typhoons.
