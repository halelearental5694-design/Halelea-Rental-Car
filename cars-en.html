FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Vehicles

Which vehicle do you recommend?

For 2–3 people, we recommend a kei car. For groups of 4 or more, a wagon is the best choice. If you're looking for a unique and memorable experience, our three-wheel trike is also very popular.

Can I drive the trike with a standard driver's license?

Yes. Our three-wheel trikes can be driven with a valid standard driver's license in Japan. No helmet is required.

How much luggage can each vehicle hold?

A kei car can accommodate approximately two suitcases, while a wagon can carry around five to six suitcases.

Do all vehicles support Bluetooth?

Bluetooth is available on selected vehicles only. If you require this feature, please let us know when making your reservation.
