ROUTE

Access Information

Here are the directions to our store from major landmarks.

✈ From Ishigaki Airport

About 25 minutes by car. A paid shuttle service to/from Ishigaki Airport is available for customers with reservations. Shuttle service requires advance booking, so please request it when making your reservation.

⛴ From the Ferry Terminal

About 5 minutes by car. Conveniently located for picking up or returning your vehicle before and after island-hopping tours. Paid shuttle service is also available.

🚗 Access from Downtown

About 5 minutes by car from Euglena Mall or the 730 Crossing. Conveniently located with easy access from major hotels.

PICK UP

About Our Paid Shuttle Service

We offer paid shuttle services to and from Ishigaki Airport and the Ferry Terminal. If you would like to use this service, please let us know when making your reservation. Please note that shuttle requests may not be available depending on traffic conditions or booking availability on the day.

AROUND

Nearby Attractions

There are convenient facilities around our store to visit before or after your drive and sightseeing.

⛽ Gas station

There are convenient gas stations located near our store for refueling before returning your vehicle. Please return the vehicle with a full tank of fuel. *3-wheel trikes do not require refueling before return.

🏪 Convenience store

There is a convenience store near the shop, making it convenient to pick up items before your drive.

🍴 Restaurant

Located close to downtown with convenient access to popular local restaurants serving Ishigaki beef, Yaeyama sofa, and fresh seafood.

FAQ

Access FAQ

Questions about access and shuttle services.

Is parking available?

Yes, we have parking space available for customers.

Can I take a taxi to the store?

Yes. Taxis are available from Ishigaki Airport, downtown, and the Ferry Terminal.

How long does it take from Ishigaki Airport?

It usually takes about 25 minutes by car. Travel times may vary depending on the time of day and traffic conditions.

Can I walk from the Ferry Terminal?

As it is a bit too far to walk, we recommend taking a car, taxi, or using our shuttle service.

Do I need to reserve the shuttle service?

Yes. Our paid shuttle service requires advance reservation, so please request it when making your booking.

Please feel free to contact us.

If you have any questions about access or shuttle services, please feel free to contact us via LINE or phone.
