Ishigaki Island Car Rental Guide

We have put together a helpful guide covering everything from the reservation process to returning your rental car, along with useful driving tips, so that even first-time visitors renting a car in Ishigaki Island can enjoy a safe and worry-free trip.

LINE How It Works
  • Reliable support from reservation to return
  • Airport & Ferry Terminal Transfers (Paid / Advanced Consultation Required)
  • We also provide information on Ishigaki Island drive routes and local spots.
  • We accept International Driving Permits (IDP).

POINT

Please Read Before Using Our Service

Please review these notes before your rental to ensure a safe and comfortable drive around Ishigaki Island.

  • Please present the driver's licenses for all driver(s).
  • Please check the vehicle for any existing scratches or dents together with our staff before departure.
  • Please wear seatbelts in all seats.
  • Child safety seats require advance reservation (1,000 yen each).
  • Drunk driving and dangerous driving are strictly prohibited.。
  • Please obey Japanese traffic laws and drive safely.

GAS STATION

Refueling before returning the car

  • There are gas stations, such as ENEOS, located around our branch. Please return the vehicle with a full tank of gas from a nearby gas station.
  • Please present your refueling receipt to our staff upon return.
  • Gasoline is included for 3-wheel trikes.
  • For trikes, please refuel at least 1 liter only when the fuel light turns on.
EMERGENCY

What to Do in Case of an Accident or Breakdown

  • If there are any injured people, please call 119 immediately as your top priority.
  • In the event of an accident, please be sure to report it to the police (110).
  • After that, please contact HaleLea Rent-a-Car at 070-1220-5694.
  • Please do not settle any claims directly with other parties without consulting us.
  • If you notice any abnormality with the vehicle, please stop driving immediately and contact us.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are some of the most frequently asked questions before your rental.

Can I make a same-day reservation?

Yes. Same-day reservations are available depending on vehicle availability.

Do you provide airport or ferry terminal pickup?

Paid pickup service is available from New Ishigaki Airport and the Remote Island Ferry Terminal. Please contact us in advance.

Do you offer child seats?

Yes. Child seats are available for ¥1,000 each. Advance reservation is required.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit cards, transportation IC cards, PayPay, Rakuten Pay, au PAY and QUICPay.

What should I do if an accident occurs?

First, please call 110 or 119, and then contact HaleLea Rent-a-Car.

Can I return the vehicle outside of operating hours?

Please contact us prior to your rental if you need an after-hours return.
